# Internal linking

## Hierarchy (template)

In `src/pages/รับซื้อโน๊ตบุ๊ค/[slug].astro`:

- Series pages → brand hub + child model pages (up to 8)
- Model pages → brand hub + parent series + `/` + `/ตีราคาโน๊ตบุ๊ค/`

## Body links

Series/Model markdown appends contextual links to brand, series, second-hand, price-check, and relevant guides — not a full money-page link farm from every model.
